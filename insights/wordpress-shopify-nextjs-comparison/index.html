Website strategy

WordPress, Shopify, or Next.js: which platform is right for your business?

A decision framework based on budget, content needs, e-commerce requirements, integrations, and long-term flexibility. There is no universal “best”—only the best fit for your situation.

Quick comparison

WordPress — Excellent for content-heavy sites, blogs, and flexible marketing sites. Large plugin ecosystem. Good when non-technical team members need to edit content often. Can power e-commerce via WooCommerce.

Shopify — Best when selling products is the core of the business. Strong checkout, payments, and app ecosystem. Less ideal as a pure content or custom-app platform.

Next.js (custom) — Best when you need maximum performance, unique UX, complex integrations, or a product that will evolve into a web application. Higher initial investment, greater long-term control.

Decision questions

  • Do you primarily need content pages and lead forms, or product sales?
  • How often will non-developers update content or products?
  • Do you need deep custom workflows or third-party system integrations?
  • What is your realistic budget and timeline for launch and maintenance?
  • Will this site likely grow into a more complex product in 1–2 years?

Typical recommendations

Most SMEs in Bangladesh starting out do well with a well-structured WordPress or Shopify site that has strong SEO foundations. Custom Next.js becomes the better investment when the business has outgrown templates or needs product-like features.

We help you choose based on your actual goals—not on what is trendy.
