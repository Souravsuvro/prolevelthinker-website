SEO foundations

How to plan service pages that can earn organic traffic.

Build useful pages around customer questions, proof, location relevance, and clear next steps. Ranking is a side effect of being the most helpful result for the searcher.

1. Start with real search intent

List the questions and problems your ideal customers type into Google. Group them into core services and supporting topics. Each high-intent service should eventually own a dedicated page.

2. Structure that answers and converts

A strong service page typically includes:

  • Clear headline that matches the search
  • Who it’s for and the main outcome
  • How the process works
  • Proof (case notes, numbers, testimonials when available)
  • Location / service-area relevance where appropriate
  • FAQs that address remaining objections
  • One primary call to action

3. Technical and on-page basics

Unique title and meta description, sensible heading hierarchy, internal links from related pages, fast loading, and mobile-friendly layout. These are table stakes—not differentiators.

4. Measure and improve

Track organic traffic, rankings for target terms, and—most importantly—form submissions or calls from those pages. Iterate based on what actually produces leads.
