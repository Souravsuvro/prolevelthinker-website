Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ProLevelThinker (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a project inquiry or contact form, we collect the details you provide (such as name, business name, email, phone/WhatsApp, service interest, budget range, timeline, and project description). We may also collect basic technical data such as browser type and IP address for security and analytics.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to your inquiry, prepare estimates or recommendations, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and retention

Inquiry data is retained as long as needed to handle your request and for legitimate business records. You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Third parties

We may use trusted tools for email, analytics, or form processing. Those providers process data only as needed to perform their services for us.

Your choices

You can request access to or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing us through the contact details on this website.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the contact form on our website or the email address provided in our response to your inquiry.
